Essential Tips to Avoid Scams

Secure Your Accounts

Use strong, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication

Verify Communications

Check the sender's details before responding to emails or messages

Be Cautious with Payments

Never transfer money to unknown persons or unverified accounts

Protect Personal Info

Don't share sensitive information like passwords or banking details

Common Scams in India

UPI Payment Scams

Scammers send fake UPI payment requests or create QR codes that steal money when scanned. They often pose as legitimate businesses or service providers.

Warning signs: Unsolicited payment requests, pressure to pay immediately, fake customer care numbers.

Most common scam Youth targeted

KYC Update Scams

Scammers call or message claiming your KYC needs updating, then trick you into sharing OTPs or installing remote access apps to steal banking information.

Warning signs: Calls from unknown numbers, threats of account suspension, requests for OTP.

Widespread All age groups

Investment Scams

Fake investment schemes promising high returns through stock trading, cryptocurrency, or real estate. Often use fake celebrity endorsements.

Warning signs: Guaranteed high returns, pressure to invest quickly, fake testimonials.

High financial loss Middle-aged targeted

Job Offer Scams

Fake job offers from reputed companies asking for advance fees for processing, training, or documentation. Often target students and job seekers.

Warning signs: Unsolicited job offers, requests for advance payment, vague job descriptions.

Students targeted Urban areas

SIM Swap Scams

Scammers duplicate your SIM card to bypass two-factor authentication and gain access to your bank accounts and digital wallets.

Warning signs: Sudden loss of network, unauthorized bank transactions, SIM registration issues.

Sophisticated High net worth targets

Courier Scams

Scammers posing as courier company officials claim illegal items were found in your package and demand money to avoid legal action.

Warning signs: Calls about unknown packages, threats of police action, demands for immediate payment.

Psychological pressure Elderly targeted
