About iReport - Cyber Crime Assisant Portal

iReport is an initiative of the Cyber Crime Assisant Portal Coordination Centre (I4C) under the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India. Our mission is to provide a secure platform for citizens to report cyber crimes and access resources to protect themselves from digital threats.

Our Mission

To create a cyber-safe India by enabling citizens to report cyber crimes easily and providing timely assistance to victims.

Our Vision

A digitally empowered India where citizens can use technology without fear of cyber crimes.

Legal Framework

Operating under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and other relevant cyber laws of India.

Partnerships

Collaborating with state police, financial institutions, and cybersecurity experts to combat cyber crimes.
