Security Tips

Use Strong Passwords

Create unique passwords with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols for each account.

Enable 2FA

Add an extra layer of security with two-factor authentication on all important accounts.

Update Regularly

Keep your software, apps, and operating systems updated to patch security vulnerabilities.

Be Wary of Links

Don't click on suspicious links in emails or messages, even if they seem to be from trusted sources.
