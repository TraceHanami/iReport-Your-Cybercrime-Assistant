Cyber Crime Awareness

Phishing Awareness

Learn how to identify and avoid phishing attempts:

  • Check sender's email address carefully
  • Look for spelling and grammar mistakes
  • Never click on suspicious links
  • Verify requests for personal information
  • Use two-factor authentication
  • Keep your software updated

Online Financial Fraud

Protect yourself from banking and payment fraud:

  • Enable two-factor authentication for all financial accounts
  • Use strong, unique passwords for each banking platform
  • Monitor account activity regularly for unauthorized transactions
  • Never share OTPs, PINs, or passwords with anyone
  • Verify payment requests through multiple channels
  • Use only official banking apps from trusted sources
  • Avoid conducting financial transactions on public Wi-Fi
  • Check for HTTPS and security certificates on banking websites

Social Media Safety

Stay secure while using social platforms:

  • Review and adjust privacy settings regularly
  • Be cautious about sharing personal information publicly
  • Verify friend requests before accepting them
  • Report suspicious accounts and content immediately
  • Think carefully before posting personal details or locations
  • Use strong, unique passwords for each social media account
  • Be aware of social engineering tactics used by scammers
  • Limit location sharing and check-in features

Ransomware Protection

Prevent and respond to ransomware attacks:

  • Regularly back up important files to external drives or cloud
  • Keep your operating system and software updated
  • Use reputable antivirus and anti-malware software
  • Be extremely cautious with email attachments
  • Avoid visiting suspicious or untrustworthy websites
  • Never pay ransom demands - contact authorities instead
  • Enable pop-up blockers in your web browser
  • Educate employees about phishing and social engineering

Identity Theft Prevention

Protect your personal information from theft:

  • Shred sensitive documents before disposal
  • Use strong, unique passwords for all online accounts
  • Monitor your credit reports regularly for suspicious activity
  • Be cautious when using public Wi-Fi networks
  • Use VPN for secure internet connections
  • Freeze your credit if you suspect identity theft
  • Secure your devices with passwords or biometric locks
  • Be wary of unsolicited requests for personal information

Online Shopping Security

Safe practices for e-commerce transactions:

  • Shop only from reputable and well-known websites
  • Look for HTTPS and padlock symbol in address bar
  • Use credit cards instead of debit cards for online purchases
  • Avoid saving payment information on shopping sites
  • Check seller ratings and reviews before purchasing
  • Be cautious of deals that seem too good to be true
  • Use virtual credit cards for additional security
  • Keep records of all online transactions and confirmations

Mobile Device Security

Protecting your smartphones and tablets:

  • Use strong passwords or biometric authentication
  • Install apps only from official app stores
  • Keep your device's operating system updated
  • Be cautious of app permissions - grant only necessary ones
  • Enable remote tracking and wiping features
  • Avoid connecting to unsecured public Wi-Fi networks
  • Use mobile security software for additional protection
  • Be careful about what you download and install

Password Security

Creating and managing secure passwords:

  • Use long passwords with mix of characters, numbers, symbols
  • Avoid using personal information in passwords
  • Use different passwords for different accounts
  • Consider using a reputable password manager
  • Enable two-factor authentication wherever possible
  • Change passwords regularly, especially after security breaches
  • Never share passwords through email or messaging apps
  • Be cautious of password reset requests you didn't initiate

Public Wi-Fi Risks

Staying safe on public networks:

  • Avoid accessing sensitive information on public Wi-Fi
  • Use a VPN when connecting to public networks
  • Verify the network name with establishment staff
  • Turn off file sharing when on public networks
  • Forget the network after use to prevent auto-connection
  • Use your mobile data for sensitive transactions when possible
  • Ensure your firewall is enabled on your device
  • Look for HTTPS on websites when browsing publicly

Child Online Safety

Protecting children in digital spaces:

  • Use parental control software to monitor and restrict access
  • Educate children about online risks and safe behavior
  • Keep computers in common areas of the home
  • Set privacy settings on social media and gaming accounts
  • Teach children not to share personal information online
  • Monitor your child's online friends and contacts
  • Encourage open communication about online experiences
  • Set time limits for device and internet usage

About iReport - Cyber Crime Assistant Portal

iReport is an initiative of the Cyber Crime Assistant Portal Coordination Centre (I4C) under the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India. Our mission is to provide a secure platform for citizens to report cyber crimes and access resources to protect themselves from digital threats.

Our Mission

To create a cyber-safe India by enabling citizens to report cyber crimes easily and providing timely assistance to victims.

Our Vision

A digitally empowered India where citizens can use technology without fear of cyber crimes.

Legal Framework

Operating under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and other relevant cyber laws of India.

Partnerships

Collaborating with state police, financial institutions, and cybersecurity experts to combat cyber crimes.
