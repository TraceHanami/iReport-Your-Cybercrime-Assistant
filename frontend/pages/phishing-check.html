Common Phishing Indicators

Suspicious URLs
URLs that don't match the claimed organization, use IP addresses instead of domain names, or have misspellings of legitimate domains.
Urgent Requests
Messages creating a sense of urgency, threatening consequences if you don't act immediately, or offering too-good-to-be-true deals.
Spoofed Sender Information
Emails that appear to be from legitimate organizations but have slight variations in the sender's email address or display name.
Requests for Credentials
Messages asking you to provide passwords, PINs, or other sensitive information, often with a link to a fake login page.
Suspicious Attachments
Unexpected attachments, especially with unusual file extensions like .exe, .scr, or .zip that may contain malware.
Poor Grammar & Spelling
Messages with obvious grammatical errors, spelling mistakes, or awkward phrasing that professional organizations would avoid.
