About Us

We are India's premier digital safety initiative — dedicated to protecting citizens, businesses, and organizations from evolving cyber threats. Our mission is to build awareness, empower users, and promote a secure online environment across the nation through cutting-edge technology and collaborative efforts.

Our Mission

To create a safe digital ecosystem for every Indian by spreading cybersecurity awareness, detecting cyber threats in real-time, and enabling rapid response through seamless collaboration with national and international cybercrime agencies. We strive to make India a global leader in cybersecurity resilience.

Our Vision

To establish India as a global cybersecurity powerhouse where every citizen can connect, work, transact, and innovate online with absolute confidence and safety. We envision a digitally empowered India protected against all forms of cyber threats through proactive measures and citizen awareness.

Who We Are

A specialized task force comprising cybersecurity experts, digital forensics professionals, legal advisors, law enforcement officers, and dedicated volunteers working in synergy to combat online fraud, data theft, cyber harassment, and financial crimes. Our team operates 24/7 to ensure rapid response to cyber incidents.

Our Partners

We collaborate with government departments, state police cyber cells, financial institutions, technology firms, academic institutions, and international cybersecurity organizations to strengthen India's cyber defense infrastructure and share threat intelligence for a safer digital India.
